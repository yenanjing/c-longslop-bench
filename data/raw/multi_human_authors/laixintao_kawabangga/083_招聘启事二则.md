我就职的公司东海集团（Shopee 母公司）拓展 AI 云业务（neocloud），现在招聘计算、存储、网络方面的人才，是一个进入 AI Infra 领域很好的机会，以下是一个初级岗位和一个高级岗位的职位介绍。年限并不是硬性要求，有兴趣都可以发来简历试一试，邮箱是博客侧边栏的地址。这两个岗位目前都没有工作签证，要求应聘者必须在新加坡且有 PR 或 SC。

其一

Site Reliability Engineer, Compute and Storage (Graduate)

Level: Graduate / 0–2 years · Location: Singapore

About the team

Sea Cloud gives businesses and developers the infrastructure to build, train and run AI at scale: GPU compute, storage, networking, managed Kubernetes and model APIs, delivered as a public cloud.

The Compute and Storage SRE team delivers and provisions the platform’s foundation products and keeps them available, fast and durable: virtual machines, managed Kubernetes, block storage, object storage, and the traffic gateways that put them in front of customers. We combine software and systems engineering: we build the automation and observability that run these services, define and defend service-level objectives, write the standard operating procedures (SOPs) the platform runs on, and lead the response when things go wrong. We also prove resilience rather than assume it, through chaos engineering and platform-level disaster-recovery fire drills, and we harden the platform’s security and strengthen its compliance controls.

Join us to help build and run a cloud platform, where your work shapes how the service is run for years to come.

What you will do

Help operate the compute and storage products: virtual machines, managed Kubernetes, block storage, object storage and traffic gateways.

Deliver and provision infrastructure products: bring up new hosts, hypervisors, Kubernetes clusters and storage clusters, and take them from install through acceptance tests to service, using automated pipelines.

Implement improvements to reliability, efficiency and security, from small fixes to whole features, working from designs reviewed with senior engineers.

Build and tune dashboards and alerts for hypervisors, Kubernetes clusters, storage clusters and traffic gateways.

Write code (Python or Go) that automates operational work: health checks, capacity reports, node and volume lifecycle steps, runbook actions.

Turn each repeated manual fix into an SOP, then a script, then an automated remediation.

Write and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs) for routine operations and known failures, and test that they work as written.

Take part in chaos experiments and disaster-recovery fire drills: prepare the run sheet, watch the signals, record what broke and what recovered.

Apply security hardening and compliance work: patch hosts and clusters, fix findings from vulnerability scans and hardening baselines, and collect the evidence audits need.

Respond to alerts, collect evidence, and contribute to incident reviews.

What you need

A degree in computer science, computer engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Coding ability in at least one of Python, Go, C or Java, shown through coursework, internships or projects.

Working knowledge of Linux: processes, file systems, systemd, and the shell.

The basics of networking (TCP/IP, DNS, routing) and of how operating systems manage memory and I/O.

A habit of debugging from evidence: logs, metrics and reproduction, not guessing.

Clear written English; incident reviews and runbooks are part of the job.

Logical reasoning and critical thinking: you question assumptions, weigh the evidence, and can explain your conclusions.

Strong ownership: you see a problem through to the fix, not just to the handoff.

Eagerness to learn: you pick up new systems quickly and keep growing your skills.

Nice to have

An internship or project with containers, Kubernetes, Terraform or Ansible.

Exposure to virtualization (KVM, QEMU) or to a storage system such as Ceph, MinIO or ZFS.

Exposure to Prometheus, Grafana or another monitoring stack.

A homelab, open-source contribution or competitive programming record.

其二

Sea Cloud — Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Compute and Storage

Level: Senior / 6+ years

Location: Singapore

About the team

Sea Cloud gives businesses and developers the infrastructure to build, train and run AI at scale: GPU compute, storage, networking, managed Kubernetes and model APIs, delivered as a public cloud.

The Compute and Storage SRE team delivers and provisions the platform’s foundation products and keeps them available, fast and durable: virtual machines, managed Kubernetes, block storage, object storage, and the traffic gateways that put them in front of customers. We combine software and systems engineering: we build the automation and observability that run these services, define and defend service-level objectives, write the standard operating procedures (SOPs) the platform runs on, and lead the response when things go wrong. We also prove resilience rather than assume it, through chaos engineering and platform-level disaster-recovery fire drills, and we harden the platform’s security and strengthen its compliance controls.

Join us to help build and run a cloud platform, where your work shapes how the service is run for years to come.

What you will do

Own the reliability of one or more compute and storage products, such as virtual machines, managed Kubernetes, block storage, object storage or traffic gateways. You set their SLOs, agree error budgets with the product teams, and report against them.

Own the delivery and provisioning of infrastructure products: design repeatable, automated pipelines that bring up new capacity, regions and availability zones (hosts, hypervisors, Kubernetes and storage clusters, traffic gateways), with acceptance tests before anything goes into service.

Design and implement systems that continually improve the reliability, efficiency and security of the compute and storage platform, from architecture through to production code.

Review new designs and launches for failure modes, data durability, observability and recovery before they reach customers.

Design and build the reliability tooling the team runs on: automated detection and remediation, safe rollouts with canaries and automatic rollback, and capacity forecasting for compute and storage.

Own the SOP library for your products: make every SOP current, tested in drills, and automated where it can be.

Lead the chaos engineering programme: choose the failure modes that matter (host, rack, disk, network partition, gateway, control plane), design experiments with a bounded blast radius, and turn findings into fixes.

Plan and run platform-level disaster-recovery fire drills, up to the loss of an availability zone. Measure recovery against RTO and RPO targets and keep the evidence auditors will ask for.

Drive security hardening and compliance for your products: define hardening baselines for hosts, hypervisors, Kubernetes and storage (for example CIS benchmarks), run vulnerability and patch management, enforce least-privilege access and encryption, and turn compliance controls into automated checks and audit evidence.

Lead major incidents as incident commander, run blameless post-incident reviews, and drive the fixes to closure.

Mentor graduate engineers and shape the on-call rotation.

What you need

6+ years in SRE, production engineering, infrastructure software engineering, or a related field, on large distributed systems.

Strong coding in Go or Python. You have shipped production tools and services, not only scripts.

Production experience operating at least one of: a virtualization platform, Kubernetes at scale, or a distributed storage system.

Deep Linux skills: kernel, file system and block I/O behaviour, performance analysis, and debugging under pressure.

Infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar) and building an observability stack, not only using one.

A track record of leading incidents and of running failure tests or DR exercises in production.

Working knowledge of datacenter networking: L3 fabrics, BGP, load balancing, DNS.
